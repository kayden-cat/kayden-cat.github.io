This artwork represents a child, who should be growing in a healthy environment, getting unfiltered exposure to the internet. Even before his consciousness has fully formed, he is exposed to a false and edited realm of reality, which renders his ability to form a true sense of self, illustrated by the wavering child-like silhouette being consumed by the glitches.
