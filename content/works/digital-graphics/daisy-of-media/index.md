This work represents the corruption and destruction of childhood due to social media. The daisy represents purity, innocence, and childhood. While it's connected to the phone, the glitches travel upwards, rapidly damaging and corrupting the purity of the daisy. I illustrated how childhood gets lost through placing tainted pellets that are half pixelated, dying, and falling to the ground, resulting from the harmful effects of social media. The phone screen is filled with a distorted and almost virus-like, broken background, with simple pixelated symbols that represent the dangers of social media and the harms it can do. The monotone black and white colors symbolize depletion of hope and childhood, along with the surrounding glitch-like rectangle blocks, to further create a sense of immediate danger. The shadow around the daisy not only brings focus to the center but implies the soon coming doom in a slow and ominous way.
