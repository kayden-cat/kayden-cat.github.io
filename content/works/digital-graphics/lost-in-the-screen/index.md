This work represents the sensation of being overwhelmed by excessive media stimuli and notifications. In the front of the screen, I used perspective to depict the phone as if it is protruding forward, and illustrated how the excessive amount of notifications cover the screen.

The figure below that is glitching with pink glitch effects symbolizes someone who is overloaded by the excessive stimuli, further emphasized by the falling posture and the static like texture, illustrating a state of paralysis.
