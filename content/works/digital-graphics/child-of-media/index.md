This work represents a child’s sense of identity that has been exposed by social media from childhood. Unlike the previous generation that was able to grow up without being exposed to social media, and encountered it later on, today’s generation lives in an era where media exposure is unavoidable. I portrayed how growing up in such an environment leads a child to adopt the influences of online culture as a natural part of life. To communicate this, I used a digitalized silhouette of the child with a mechanical gradient, depicting the shadow as becoming digitalized.
