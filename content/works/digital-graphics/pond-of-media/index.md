This artwork represents how childhood, something that is meant to stay pure, is being polluted by the artificial waves of social media. The neon purple, orange, and pink hued currents are sweeping the beautiful pond along with other artificial stimuli like social media icons. I distorted the children’s toys with a mosaic effect, to illustrate that a pure childhood that is meant to be enjoyed is being polluted.
