This work, Space of Media, represents the loss of childhood innocence and simplicity due to social media. The colorful playroom filled with child-like toys and vibrant colors that symbolize childhood innocence contrasts with the ominous digital space where the monstrous creature resides.  
In order to illustrate the monster as a digital and otherworldly being, I collaged it in a flat-two dimensional way, and used liner pens to reinforce its artificial / unreal quality. 

The creature stretches out its tentacles towards the child, while drooling out phrases commonly seen in social media, further highlighting the dangers of social media influence.

I used the falling toy blocks and the letters written “Danger” as an explicit warning towards the audience.
