---
title: "Neglection"
year: "2026"
medium: "Acrylic markers, acrylic paint, pen, and cellophane on canvas"
dimensions: "45.47 x 45.47 x 1.50 cm"
weight: 7
---
This work is supposed to represent the problematic situation of certain parents neglecting their children. A clear contrast is shown between the two reflections. On the right side, the mother is taking selfies of herself, with more items, lighting, and focus on her, which show priority on herself over her child. On the left, the child is crying in the corner, with the theme of neglect reinforced through the disparate, dull, gloomy, lighting, and the single rattle toy. 

The way this whole scene is posted on social media makes it seem more ironic, satirical, and depressing. It also shows the problematic reality of how little attention people pay attention to this issue through the likes and reaction effects received by the parent.
