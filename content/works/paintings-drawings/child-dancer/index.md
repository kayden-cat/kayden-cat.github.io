In order to call out parents monetizing their children for profit, I represented a young child dancing on a stage that is based on a famous social media interface. I illustrated an expressionless young child attached to a string of wires, being controlled by her parents, like a marionette. This symbolizes how this act of profit feels extremely forceful and exploitative towards the young child. I used color to contrast the parent against a parent who prioritizes their child’s well being.
