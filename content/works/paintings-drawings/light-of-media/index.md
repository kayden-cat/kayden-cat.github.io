Light of Media is a work that explores childhood loss resulting from social media. I used contrast to represent the influence social media has on childhood, with one of them being brightness. To emphasize on the child and the ray of light, I highlighted the stream of light and toned down the background, placing focus on childhood play activities. Inside the stream of light, I placed social media apps blending with, and covering iconic places of the childhood to represent the influence of social media on children today. The child-like shadow playfully holding balloons serve as a contrast to the current state of the child and acts a reminder that reminisces childhood innocence that is now lost.
