This artwork represents the everlasting effects of social media influence on one’s development. Starting from a very young age, as the child grows up to become a teenager, he is non-stop exposed to social media, to the point where it overshadows his childhood, peer to peer interaction, and his life. The black shadow represents what is forever corrupted and cannot be recovered. The pixelated social media panels invading the screen are composed of popular memes, representing the corruption of his mind and what he prioritizes over what is real. The monotone background and the black shadow in the right represents what is forever corrupted and cannot be recovered. I illustrated this scene to depict social media addiction throughout childhood, and further emphasized it by distorting the people’s face next to him with a glitch-like effect, rendering their faces unrecognizable.
