I intended to illustrate how much social media interlocks with an individual’s life. I reflected the growth of a child by depicting it through a film roll. Furthermore, through illustrating social media icons spilling out of a phone and overlaying them across different scenes, I intended to represent how social media can influence one’s entire life.
