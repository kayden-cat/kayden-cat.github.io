---
title: "Wall of Media"
year: "2026"
medium: "Acrylic markers, pen, acrylic paint, cellophane on paper"
dimensions: "39.37 x 54.61 x 0 cm"
weight: 12
---
This work represents isolation resulting from social media. In the center, we can see a child on his phone, sending memes, while being surrounded by a wall composed of social media icons. This symbolizes how children after getting repeated exposure end up being addicted to social media. Even though the two boys on the sides are urging the boy in the center to play with them, he stays isolated within his own realm of social media, refusing to participate in prominent childhood activities like soccer and playing with toys. I used color to make a clear contrast: while the dimension in the center remains dark and reserved, the sides are colorful and filled with childhood elements. The negative space in the center was filled with black to create a deeper sense of isolation and darkness as if the child is in a closed room. I intended to convey a message of how social media can induce anxiety by depicting the messages of social media in an unstable form constantly demanding engagement.
