This work represents the reality of parents that force their children to be influencers and use them for monetization. One prominent inspiration behind this work was a “kidfluencer” that was forced by his parents to grow up on camera, while producing millions of dollars in profit. In order to represent the child being exploited, I depicted cameras aggressively taking photos of the child, while he is frowning. To emphasize how disturbing and unnatural monetization of child media is, I placed a simple and smooth textured green screen with an artificial color that stands out. The lifeless stack of toys under the array of live reactions signify a loss of childhood joy and innocence, further reflected in the expression of the child.
