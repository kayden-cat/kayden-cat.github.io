This work explores water beyond its physical form, expanding its meaning through abstract interpretation and artistic questioning. Through exploring various instances and forms of water, from familiar domestic settings to natural bodies of water, the work questions the purpose behind its circulation and strives to find meaning behind its movement. Although water is something that constantly exists around, within, and amidst us through our daily lives, its presence becomes something we accept without question. Using familiar surroundings as a point of departure, the work expands the representation of water beyond its physical existence, contemplating its movement as a reflection of change, existence, and the relationship between things and their surroundings.
