This installation work explores the boundary between reality and simulation, how reality, our perception can be fabricated in digital or social media. By combining artificial plants, phones playing videos of nature, and a projection of natural imagery on PVC films, I construct an intentionally artificial environment that imitates the appearance of reality. As I enter the installation, I am immersed in the green light from the projection, and surrounded by artificial plants, creating an illusion of a forest constructed entirely by fabricated elements. The hands reaching towards the digital images of nature further emphasizes the distance between reality and artificial representation, encouraging the audience to question whether the reality they perceive is an authentic experience or merely an artificial construction. Through these layers of imitation, I intend to portray social media as an echo chamber where fabricated representations continue to reinforce themselves, creating a closed environment that attempts to resemble reality, without being true. I invite viewers to question what is real, how media manipulates our perception, and whether something false can feel authentic when it becomes the only reality that we experience.
