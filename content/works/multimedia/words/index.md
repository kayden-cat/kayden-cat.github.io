This artwork is an interactive sound-visual installation work made through using the program TouchDesigner. In order to keep my own private records, I combined morse code, textese, and custom symbols, to create my own secret language. As the audience enters the space of the work, the orderly arrangement of characters immediately collides and scatters into unpredictable, and abstract, disorderly forms. This experience allows the audience to perceive movement through their senses and engage with emotion prior to language before understanding.
