Hi, I'm Ohyun Kwon (Kayden), a student at Seoul Scholars International Art and Design in Seoul, Korea. Most of my work is about growing up with social media — how screens, likes, and endless scrolling change what childhood looks and feels like. I grew up in that world myself, so I want my art to ask questions about it instead of just accepting it.

I work across painting, digital illustration, and multimedia installation. I use acrylic markers, paint, pens, and collage on canvas and paper, and I've also started building interactive pieces with TouchDesigner and making video works about things we see every day, like water.

Outside the studio, I founded and lead my school's Photoshop and Design Club, where I teach other students, and I lead our American Sign Language Club. I've edited and acted in our school films "Drawn In" and "RES-N8," and I've worked as a translator and guide at art fairs, galleries, and the Kimsechoong Museum. Being around artists and visitors from all over the world showed me how art can connect people even when they don't share a language.

I want to keep making art that makes people stop scrolling for a moment and look a little closer.
